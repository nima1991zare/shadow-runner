# Store Release Checklist

## Suggested listing

**English short description:** Outsmart the moving sun. Cross 1,000 paths where only the shadows keep you alive.

**Persian title:** دونده سایه

**Persian short description:** از خورشید متحرک فرار کن؛ هزار مسیر را فقط در امنیت سایه‌ها پشت سر بگذار.

**Category:** Puzzle / Adventure

**Recommended model:** Paid download with no advertising for version 1.0. This fits the focused single-player experience and avoids integrating several incompatible store billing SDKs before launch.

## Before submission

- Choose and register the final app ID and publisher name.
- Commission or create final music, sound effects, character art, environment models, and licensed fonts.
- Produce icon, feature graphic, phone/tablet screenshots, gameplay trailer, and Persian/English descriptions.
- Add a public privacy policy URL even though version 1.0 collects no personal data.
- Complete age-rating questionnaires and disclose the mild fantasy hazard.
- Test all 1,000 seeds, save migration, interruptions, orientation, offline mode, and low-memory recovery on physical devices.
- Run Android closed testing and iOS TestFlight before production.
- Sign release artifacts and retain signing keys in secure offline backups.
- Verify current Cafe Bazaar, Myket, App Store, and Microsoft Store rules immediately before submission.

## Version 1.1 candidates

- Hand-crafted story chapters and cinematic sequences
- Cloud save and achievements
- Controller support and accessibility remapping
- Daily challenge with a shared deterministic seed
- Cosmetic outfits inspired by distinct historical regions, reviewed for cultural accuracy
